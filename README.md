# Photo2Video Archive — user documentation

Create HEVC archive videos from selected photos and restore frames or available originals.

[Official product website](https://pixel-vault.arte-soft.com/) · [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760718313) · [Side-by-side comparisons](https://pixel-vault.arte-soft.com/compare/) · [Guides & workflows](https://pixel-vault.arte-soft.com/articles/) · [Full LLM reference (llms-full.txt)](https://pixel-vault.arte-soft.com/llms-full.txt) · [Arte Soft EOOD](https://arte-soft.com/)

## Who this is for

People organizing locally available photo batches who understand the difference between lossy frames and exact originals.

## At a glance

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive video | Selected photos become frames in a verified HEVC MP4 archive saved to Photos. | HEVC frame export is lossy; compression varies by input. No fixed savings ratio is promised. |
| Frame export | Open a valid archive and export selected frames as images. | Decoded frames are not byte-exact originals. |
| Exact restoration | The current site describes a local originals package for byte-exact restoration, names, dates and Live Photo videos. | Deleting the app removes that package. The video alone does not guarantee exact originals or all original media properties. |
| Selection and storage | Locally available photos are selected; the site says iCloud-only items are skipped. | Keeping the originals package also consumes space. Photos iCloud synchronization follows Apple settings. |
